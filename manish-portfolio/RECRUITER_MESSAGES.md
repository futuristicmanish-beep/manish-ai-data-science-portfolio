## IMPORTANT COMMUNICATION GUIDELINES

### DO's

✅ **Personalize every message** — Mention specific details about the company/person  
✅ **Be concise** — Respect their time, get to the point quickly  
✅ **Be specific** — Reference specific projects, technologies, or roles  
✅ **Be professional** — Use proper grammar, spelling, and formatting  
✅ **Be honest** — Never exaggerate your skills or experience  
✅ **Follow up appropriately** — Once after 1-2 weeks, then move on  
✅ **Express genuine interest** — Show you've researched the company  
✅ **Provide value** — Share your portfolio, show what you've built  

### DON'Ts

❌ **Don't spam** — Sending the same generic message to 50 people  
❌ **Don't beg** — Never sound desperate, maintain professional tone  
❌ **Don't lie** — Never claim false experience or skills  
❌ **Don't be pushy** — Respect if someone doesn't respond  
❌ **Don't use informal language** — Avoid "hey" "sup" or emojis in initial messages  
❌ **Don't send without proofreading** — Typos hurt your credibility  
❌ **Don't attach unsolicited large files** — Link to portfolio instead  
❌ **Don't follow up excessively** — One follow-up is enough for most situations  

---

## TIMING GUIDELINES

**When to Send:**
- **Email Applications:** Tuesday-Thursday, 8-11 AM local time (higher open rates)
- **LinkedIn Messages:** Tuesday-Thursday, during business hours
- **Follow-Ups:** Exactly 7-14 days after initial contact
- **Thank-You Emails:** Within 24 hours of interview

**How Long to Wait:**
- **After Application:** Wait 7-14 days before following up
- **After Follow-Up:** If no response after 2-4 weeks, move on
- **After Interview:** Thank-you email within 24 hours
- **After Rejection:** Can reach out again after 6-12 months

## PERSONALIZATION CHECKLIST

Before sending ANY message:

- [ ] Correct recipient name (spelling matters!)
- [ ] Correct company name
- [ ] Correct role title
- [ ] Specific mention of company/person's work
- [ ] Relevant project/skill highlighted
- [ ] Professional email signature
- [ ] Links tested and working
- [ ] Proofread for typos
- [ ] Appropriate tone for context
- [ ] Clear call-to-action (what do you want them to do?)

---

## RESPONSE RATE EXPECTATIONS

**Realistic expectations:**
- **Cold Emails:** 5-15% response rate
- **LinkedIn Connection Requests:** 30-50% acceptance rate
- **LinkedIn Messages:** 10-20% response rate
- **Referral Requests:** 30-40% positive response
- **Follow-Ups After Interview:** 90%+ response rate

**If response rates are lower:**
- Review message quality and personalization
- Check timing of messages
- Ensure links work and portfolio is impressive
- Consider if you're targeting appropriate roles
- Adjust strategy based on patterns

---

**Remember:** Quality over quantity. One well-crafted, personalized message is worth more than ten generic templates. Always be professional, honest, and respectful of people's time.
