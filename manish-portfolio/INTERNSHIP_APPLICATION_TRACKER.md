## APPLICATION STATUS CODES

- **📝 DRAFT** — Application prepared but not yet submitted
- **✉️ SUBMITTED** — Application submitted, waiting for response
- **📧 FOLLOW-UP SENT** — Follow-up email sent
- **📞 SCREENING** — Initial phone/video screening scheduled
- **💻 TECHNICAL** — Technical interview/assessment scheduled
- **🤝 FINAL** — Final round interview scheduled
- **✅ OFFER** — Offer received
- **❌ REJECTED** — Application rejected
- **⏸️ ON HOLD** — Company put hiring on hold
- **🤷 NO RESPONSE** — No response after follow-up

## NOTES & REMINDERS

- **Application Best Time:** Early morning or Tuesday-Thursday (higher response rates)
- **Follow-Up Timing:** 7-14 days after application
- **Response Time:** Companies may take 2-4 weeks to respond
- **Rejection is Normal:** Most applications don't result in interviews
- **Focus on Learning:** Every application teaches you something
- **Quality over Quantity:** 2-4 well-researched applications per week is better than 20 generic ones

---

## TIPS FOR SUCCESS

1. **Be Honest:** Never exaggerate experience or skills
2. **Be Consistent:** Ensure resume, LinkedIn, and portfolio all match
3. **Be Specific:** Tailor each application to the specific role
4. **Be Timely:** Apply early in the internship season
5. **Be Persistent:** Follow up appropriately, don't give up after rejections
6. **Be Professional:** Use professional email address and communication
7. **Be Prepared:** Research company before applying
8. **Be Patient:** Responses can take weeks
